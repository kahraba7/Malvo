What we collect

This website has no accounts and sets no cookies. The contact form does not send data to a server: it opens your email app with your message, and nothing is sent until you press send there. If you email us, we receive the name, address and message you choose to include.

How we use it

We use your message only to reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google when the page loads. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs.
