Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

This website has no accounts and sets no cookies of its own. It has no form that sends data to a server. The “Book a demo”, “Let’s Talk” and WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp with a message ready for you, and nothing is sent until you press send there. If you message or email us, we receive the name, number or address and the message you choose to include.

How we use it

We use your message only to reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project or demo. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing.

Third-party services

Fonts are served from our own domain. WhatsApp is run by Meta, which handles messages sent through it under its own terms and privacy policy. The Kanzel demo on our case-study pages shows the live site kanzel-eg.vercel.app inside the page; when it loads, that site receives your IP address and browser details and may set its own cookies. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete any email or WhatsApp correspondence we hold about you.
