Questions owners ask

Do guests need to install an app?

No. They scan a QR or tap a link and it opens in the browser.

Does it work for cafés and for groups with several branches?

Yes. A café uses the same menu, QR and booking. A group manages every branch and brand from one place.

Can it look like my restaurant?

Yes. Your name, logo, colours, dishes and prices. Each client gets their own look.

How long until we are live?

You send the menu, logo and number of tables. We build, test with your team, then you go live.

What if my staff are not technical?

Each screen does one job and uses large buttons. We train your team on your own menu.

Do we have to replace our cashier (POS) system?

No. MALVO works alongside it. Before we build, we check with you how far to connect: menu and prices, orders to the cashier, or sales reports.

Do I have to change everything at once?

No. Start with the menu and bookings, add orders when you are ready.
