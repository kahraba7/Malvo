Questions agencies ask

Whose name does the client see?

Their own. Each restaurant gets its own name, logo, colours, dishes and prices.

How is it priced?

Per project. You tell us the restaurant and what it needs, and we send a price for that client.

Can we start with just one client?

Yes. Each project is agreed on its own, so you can start with one restaurant and add more later.

What do you need from the restaurant?

Their menu and prices, logo, number of tables, and the cashier system they use, if any.

Does the restaurant have to replace its cashier (POS) system?

No. It works alongside it. Before we build, we check with the client how far to connect.

What happens after we go live?

We keep supporting the system and updating it as the restaurant’s needs change.
